## Rule lifecycle required

Every clinical rule needs an ID, clinical question, population/exclusions, source and publication date, evidence grade where available, jurisdiction/adaptation note, logic and output, safety caveats, author, independent reviewer, clinical approver, effective date, review date, superseded version, test scenarios and rollback record.

The source recommends review at least every six months and immediately after material guideline alerts; the clinical owner must approve the final cadence.

## Human review boundary

- Automated logic may organize data, flag missing evidence and propose draft items.
- Only authorized clinical staff can validate a result, interpret findings, approve a health picture or publish a roadmap.
- The system must identify the reviewer, evidence version, decision time, rationale and any override.
- Members must see plain-language limitations and the next safe action.
- Medication reminders cannot alter dose, duration or medical advice.
