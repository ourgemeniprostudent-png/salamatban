## Decisions required before handling production health data

| Area | Decision/evidence required | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Service characterization | Confirm whether each workflow is guidance, coordination, telehealth or medical practice and identify applicable licenses | **Requires legal approval** |
| Corporate authority | Contracting entity, registrations, authorized signatories, trademarks and domain ownership | **Requires business and legal approval** |
| Clinical responsibility | Responsible medical professional, provider credentials, duty boundaries and indemnity | **Requires clinical and legal approval** |
| Privacy notice | Controller/processor roles, purposes, data categories, recipients, rights and contact path | **Requires legal approval** |
| Consent | Separate versions for service, clinical processing, partner sharing, communications, research/analytics if any | **Requires legal and clinical approval** |
| Hosting jurisdiction | Iran-hosted proposal, cross-border access, backup location and sub-processors | **Requires legal approval** and vendor evidence |
| Retention/correction/deletion | Schedule by record type, legal holds, audit retention and response procedure | **Requires legal approval** |
| Terms of service | Eligibility, limitations, emergencies, suspension, acceptable use and dispute process | **Requires legal approval** |
| Commerce | Approved prices, taxes/invoices, refund/cancellation, failed service and subscription terms | **Requires business and legal approval** |
| Marketing claims | Health, outcome, availability, security and partner claims with substantiation | **Requires clinical and legal approval** |
| Vendor/partner contracts | DPA, confidentiality, security, breach notice, SLA, deletion, audit, liability and termination/export | **Requires legal approval** |
| Children/vulnerable users | Whether excluded from MVP and how age/guardian status is verified | **Requires clinical and legal approval** |
| Incident response | Notification duties, authority contacts, evidence preservation and communications | **Requires legal approval** |
| Record export/sharing | Identity verification, recipient liability, format, revocation and disclosure log | **Requires legal approval** |

## Prohibited assumptions

- Iran hosting alone does not establish compliance.
- A checkbox alone does not make consent informed, specific or sufficient.
- Labeling the service “not medical advice” does not remove professional duties created by actual workflows.
- Vendor marketing statements do not replace contractual security and privacy terms.
- Prototype copy, sample prices and partner logos must not be published as approved claims.

## Legal sign-off package expected

Issue-spotting memorandum; license map; approved service boundary; final Persian privacy notice/terms/consents; refund and cancellation policy; template provider/vendor agreements; data-retention schedule; incident notification matrix; approved claims register; signed launch approval with conditions.
