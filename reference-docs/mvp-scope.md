# Operational MVP scope

## Product outcome

Deliver a safe, auditable guidance-and-follow-up platform that converts a structured checkup into a clinician-approved health picture and 12-month roadmap. A member may execute the roadmap independently or purchase time-limited coordination support.

Hamyar Salamat is not an emergency service, insurer, payer, autonomous diagnostic system, or replacement for a licensed clinician.

## In scope for the first controlled launch

- Persian RTL public information pages and authenticated member portal.
- OTP-based member authentication; stronger MFA for staff.
- Versioned consent and privacy choices.
- Member profile, structured health history, medication/allergy capture and questionnaires.
- Checkup order/status flow and selected-center coordination.
- Private medical-document upload, validation, quarantine, review and versioning.
- Clinician work queue, documented review and controlled publication of health picture/roadmap.
- Roadmap actions with due windows, ownership, completion evidence and history.
- Self-managed mode and prepaid 3/6/9/12-month managed assistance.
- Hamyar checkout/payment, receipt, failure handling and reconciliation.
- Provider discovery/booking through an adapter, with manual fallback.
- SMS/notification preferences, reminders and delivery tracking.
- Operations task queue and non-clinical CRM synchronization.
- Member record view, export and explicitly granted sharing.
- Admin configuration, role management, audit review and operational reporting.
- Pilot analytics for funnel, completion, quality, incidents and unit economics.

## Out of scope until later approval

- Autonomous diagnosis, treatment selection, prescriptions or clinical chatbots.
- Emergency diagnosis/dispatch beyond approved guidance to emergency services.
- Insurance adjudication, provider payment collection or marketplace settlement.
- 24/7 clinical monitoring or guaranteed 24/7 human response.
- Wearables, continuous monitoring, DICOM exchange, national-scale interoperability or full FHIR server.
- Employer/insurer portals and broad B2B features.
- Advertising, partner product recommendations, ZCorpa/Spartina-specific programs.
- Native mobile applications; PWA is the provisional first channel.
- Multi-country deployment or hosting outside the approved jurisdiction.

## Pilot boundary

**Provisional:** adults aged 18+ in a limited geography, small invited cohort, selected checkup pathway(s), named provider network, business-hours support, and explicit escalation procedures. Cohort size, cities, support hours and clinical pathway must be approved before implementation configuration.

## Acceptance conditions for scope approval

- Business owner approves the in/out list, pilot cohort, services, pricing policy and support model.
- Clinical owner approves target population, questionnaires, checkup content, rules, red flags, review SLA and escalation.
- Legal reviewer approves service characterization, consent, privacy, record handling, contracts and customer policies.
- Vendors confirm eligibility, contracts, production APIs, SLAs and data-processing boundaries.
